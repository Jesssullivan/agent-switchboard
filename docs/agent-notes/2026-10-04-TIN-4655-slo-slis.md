# SLO document (R-C263)

Ruling: R-C263, TIN-4655 comment `230af90b` (operator interview
2026-10-04 ~05:05Z): define SLIs before targets, measure, then hold a
targets interview; no customer SLA.

## What changed

- `docs/operations/SLO.md`: new. Four SLIs, each with a definition, what the
  broker measures today and what must be added. Rolling 28-day reporting
  window. Explicit no-SLA statement. Gap checklist of 14 items.
- `docs/adr/0001-agent-switchboard.md`: a dated "Service level indicators"
  subsection pointing at it.

## Facts read from source at `08de158`

- `/metrics` (`:9090`, in-cluster) emits only `swb_messages_total`
  (persisted, unlabelled), `swb_mailbox_unacked` (unlabelled) and
  `swb_sessions{state="live"}`. The ADR-0002 labelled series are not emitted.
- No health endpoint, no latency histogram, no ack or expiry counters.
- `messages.created_at` is whole seconds; there is no fetch timestamp, so
  delivery latency cannot be measured today.
- Expiry is written to rows only during `inbox`; the unacked gauge filters
  by `expires_at`, so an expiry is never counted. The conservation identity
  in SLO.md needs `acked_total` and `expired_total`.
- `GET /v1/peers` with no `me` renews nothing, which makes it the
  availability probe that cannot distort the lease SLI.
